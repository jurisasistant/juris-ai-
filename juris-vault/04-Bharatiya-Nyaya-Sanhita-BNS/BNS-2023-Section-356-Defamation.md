# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 356: Criminal Defamation
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Replaces IPC Section 499 & 500*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]] • [[Shreya-Singhal-v-Union-of-India-2015]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023)
> **Section 356:** Whoever, by words either spoken or intended to be read, or by signs or by visible representations, makes or publishes any imputation concerning any person intending to harm, or knowing or having reason to believe that such imputation will harm, the reputation of such person, is said to defame that person. Punishable with imprisonment up to two (2) years, or fine, or community service.

---

## ⚖️ Old vs. New Law & Constitutional Precedents
* **Old Statutory Reference:** IPC Section 499 & 500
* **Community Service:** Section 356 introduces **community service** as an alternative statutory punishment for defamation.
* **Subramanian Swamy v. Union of India (SC 2016):** Upheld the constitutional validity of criminal defamation as a reasonable restriction under Article 19(2) protecting individual reputation and dignity under Article 21.
