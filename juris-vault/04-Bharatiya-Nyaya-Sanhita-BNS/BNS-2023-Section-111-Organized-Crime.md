# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 111: Organized Crime & Syndicates
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • First Codified Central Organized Crime Law*

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023 Section 111)
> **Section 111:** Defines organized crime as any continuing unlawful activity including kidnapping, robbery, vehicle theft, extortion, land grabbing, contract killing, economic offenses, cybercrime, or trafficking in persons/drugs committed by a syndicate using violence, threat, or financial means.

---

## ⚖️ Penalties & Precedent Standards
* **First Central Codification:** Incorporates principles from regional acts (like MCOCA) into central criminal law.
* **Penalties:** Punishable with death or life imprisonment if the offense results in death; otherwise punishable with 5 years to life imprisonment and fine not less than ₹5 Lakhs.
