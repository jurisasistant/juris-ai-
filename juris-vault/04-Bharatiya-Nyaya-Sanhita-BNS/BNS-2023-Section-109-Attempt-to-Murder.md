# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 109: Attempt to Murder
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Replaces IPC Section 307*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNS-2023-Section-103-Murder]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023)
> **Section 109:** Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge, and under such circumstances that, if he by that act caused death, he would be guilty of murder, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine; and if hurt is caused to any person by such act, the offender shall be liable either to imprisonment for life, or to such punishment as is hereinbefore mentioned.

---

## ⚖️ Evidentiary Test & Precedents
* **Old Statutory Reference:** IPC Section 307
* **State of Maharashtra v. Balram Bama Patil (SC 1983):** Intention is gathered from the weapon used, body part targeted, and severity of force applied, regardless of whether a fatal injury actually resulted.
