# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 69: Sexual Intercourse by Deceitful Promise to Marry
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Offenses Against Woman*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNS-2023-Section-63-Rape-and-Sexual-Offenses]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023 Section 69)
> *"Whoever, by deceitful means or by making promise to marry to a woman without any intention of fulfilling the same, has sexual intercourse with her, such sexual intercourse not amounting to the offence of rape, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years and shall also be liable to fine."*
> **Explanation:** "Deceitful means" shall include the false promise of employment or promotion, inducement, or marrying after suppressing identity.

---

## ⚖️ Innovation & Statutory Distinction
* **First Codified Separation:** Historically prosecuted under IPC Section 375/90 (consent under misconception of fact). BNS 2023 Section 69 creates an explicit statutory offense distinct from rape, carrying up to 10 years imprisonment.
* **Core Test:** Whether the promise to marry was false at the very inception (*bad faith from the beginning*), as distinguished from a breach of promise due to unforeseen circumstances.
