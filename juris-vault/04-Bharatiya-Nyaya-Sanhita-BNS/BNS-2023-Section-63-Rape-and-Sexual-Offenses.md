# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 63 & 64: Rape and Sexual Offenses Against Women
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Offenses Against Woman and Child*
**Connected Nodes:** [[IPC-Section-376]] • [[BNS-2023-Section-69-Deceitful-Promise-to-Marry]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[Mukesh-v-State-NCT-of-Delhi-2017]]

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023)
> **Section 63:** Defines the offense of rape, incorporating modern standards of consent—unequivocal voluntary agreement when a woman by words, gestures, or any form of non-verbal communication communicates willingness to participate in the specific sexual act.
> **Section 64:** Prescribes rigorous imprisonment of not less than ten (10) years, which may extend to imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.

---

## ⚖️ Old vs. New Law Comparison (IPC ↔ BNS)
* **Old Statutory Reference:** IPC Section 375 & 376
* **Key Innovation:** Consolidates sexual offenses under Chapter V ("Of Offences Against Woman and Child"), establishing minimum 20-year to life imprisonment for gang rape (Section 70) and mandatory victim identity protection.

## 🏛️ Landmark Supreme Court Precedents
* **Mukesh & Anr. v. State for NCT of Delhi (SC 2017):** Landmark gender justice and victim protection benchmark.
* **Independent Consent:** Absence of physical resistance does not constitute consent under explanation to Section 63.
