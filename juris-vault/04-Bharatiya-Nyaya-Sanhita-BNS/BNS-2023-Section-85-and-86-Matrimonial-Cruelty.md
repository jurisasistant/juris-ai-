# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 85 & 86: Cruelty by Husband or Relatives
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Offenses Against Woman • Replaces IPC Section 498A*
**Connected Nodes:** [[IPC-Section-498A]] • [[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]] • [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNS 2023)
> **Section 85:** Whoever, being the husband or the relative of the husband of a woman, subjects such woman to cruelty shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to fine.
> **Section 86:** Defines cruelty as any willful conduct likely to drive the woman to commit suicide or cause grave physical/mental injury, or harassment to coerce her or relatives for unlawful dowry demands.

---

## ⚖️ Old vs. New Law & Procedural Safeguards
* **Old Section:** IPC Section 498A
* **Arnesh Kumar Mandate:** Governed by mandatory police appearance notice under **[[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]]** (*Arnesh Kumar v. State of Bihar, 2014*)—prohibiting automatic police arrest for offenses punishable up to 7 years.
