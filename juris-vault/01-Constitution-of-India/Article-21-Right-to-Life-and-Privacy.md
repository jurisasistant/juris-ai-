## 📌 Text of Article 21
> *"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."*

---

## ⚖️ Evolution from "Procedure Established by Law" to Due Process
In early constitutional history (*A.K. Gopalan v. State of Madras, 1950*), Article 21 was interpreted narrowly. However, in **[[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]]**, a 7-Judge Bench of the Supreme Court revolutionized Article 21 by holding that any statutory procedure depriving life or liberty must be:
* **Just**
* **Fair**
* **Reasonable**
* **Non-Arbitrary under [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]]**

---

## 🏛️ Derivative Rights Protected under Article 21
The Supreme Court has expanded Article 21 to encompass numerous unenumerated fundamental rights:
1. **Right to Privacy** — *Declared an intrinsic Part III right by the 9-Judge Bench in [[Puttaswamy-Right-to-Privacy-2017]], leading to the enactment of the [[DPDP-Act-2023-Digital-Personal-Data-Protection]].*
2. **Right to Live with Human Dignity** — *Francis Coralie v. Union Territory of Delhi (SC 1981)*
3. **Right to Protection against Workplace Sexual Harassment** — *[[Vishaka-v-State-of-Rajasthan-1997]] & [[POSH-Act-2013]]*
4. **Right to Speedy Trial & Legal Aid** — *Hussainara Khatoon (SC 1979)*
5. **Right against Arbitrary Arrest** — *Protected by police notice rules under [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]] & [[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]]*
