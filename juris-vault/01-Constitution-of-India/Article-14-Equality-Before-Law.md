# 📜 Article 14: Equality Before Law & Non-Arbitrariness
*Part III • Fundamental Rights • The Constitution of India*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]] • [[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]] • [[Vishaka-v-State-of-Rajasthan-1997]] • [[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]]

## 📌 Text of Article 14
> *"The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India."*

---

## ⚖️ Twin Doctrines of Article 14
1. **Equality Before Law:** English common law origin; negative concept implying the absence of any special privilege in favor of any individual, and equal subjection of all classes to the ordinary law.
2. **Equal Protection of Laws:** American constitutional origin; positive concept requiring equal treatment in similar circumstances across benefits conferred and liabilities imposed.
3. **Doctrine of Reasonable Classification:** Article 14 forbids class legislation, but permits reasonable classification of persons, objects, and transactions provided:
   * It is based on an **intelligible differentia** which distinguishes persons grouped together from others left out.
   * The differentia has a **rational nexus** to the object sought to be achieved by the statute.

---

## 🏛️ Landmark Supreme Court Interpretations
* **E.P. Royappa v. State of Tamil Nadu (SC 1974):** Established the dynamic doctrine that equality and arbitrariness are sworn enemies. Wherever an administrative act is arbitrary, it is necessarily violative of [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]].
* [[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]] — *Article 14, 19, and 21 form the Golden Triangle; procedure depriving liberty must be fair, non-arbitrary, and reasonable.*
* [[Vishaka-v-State-of-Rajasthan-1997]] — *Workplace sexual harassment violates equality under Article 14 and dignity under [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]], leading to the enactment of the [[POSH-Act-2013]].*
