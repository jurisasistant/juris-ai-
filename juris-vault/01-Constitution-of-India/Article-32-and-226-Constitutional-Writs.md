## 📌 Text of Article 32
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar termed **Article 32** the "heart and soul" of the Constitution of India. It confers a **Fundamental Right** to move the Supreme Court directly for the enforcement of Part III rights.

---

## ⚖️ The 5 Constitutional Writs in India
1. **Habeas Corpus ("To have the body"):** Directs police or authority to present a detained citizen before the Court to examine legality of detention.
2. **Mandamus ("We Command"):** Commands a public official or body to perform a statutory duty that they have unlawfully refused to perform.
3. **Certiorari ("To be Certified"):** Issued by the Supreme Court or High Court to quash an order of an inferior court, tribunal, or authority acting without jurisdiction or violating natural justice.
4. **Prohibition:** Issued to stop an inferior tribunal from proceeding in a matter outside its legal jurisdiction.
5. **Quo Warranto ("By what authority?"):** Challenges the illegal appointment or holding of a public office by an unqualified person.

---

## 🏛️ Landmark Supreme Court Precedents
* **L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India (SC 7-Judge Bench 1997):** Established that the power of judicial review vested in High Courts under Article 226 and Supreme Court under Article 32 is an inviolable basic feature of the Constitution under the [[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]].
* [[Kesavananda-Bharati-v-State-of-Kerala-1973]] — *Parliament cannot amend Article 32 or abridge judicial review.*
