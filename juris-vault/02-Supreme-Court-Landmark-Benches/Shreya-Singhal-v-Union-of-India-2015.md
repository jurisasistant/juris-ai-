# 🏛️ Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015)
*Supreme Court of India • 2-Judge Bench • (2015) 5 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]] • [[BNS-2023-Section-152-Sovereignty-Protection]] • [[IT-Act-2000-Section-79]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
Struck down Section 66A of the Information Technology Act 2000 in its entirety for being unconstitutionally vague and violative of **[[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]]**.

---

## ⚖️ Key Facts & Constitutional Issues
* **Facts:** Public Interest Litigation challenging police arrests under Section 66A of the IT Act 2000 for posting critical online political speech on social media.
* **Core Issue:** Whether Section 66A (penalizing "grossly offensive" or "annoying" electronic communication) falls within reasonable restrictions under Article 19(2).

---

## 🏛️ Judgment & Ratio Decidendi
1. **Unconstitutional Vagueness:** Section 66A was struck down as it created a chilling effect on online speech without an intelligible statutory standard.
2. **Intermediary Safe Harbour:** Clarified Section 79 of the IT Act—online intermediaries (social media platforms) cannot be forced to take down content without an explicit court order or formal government notification.
