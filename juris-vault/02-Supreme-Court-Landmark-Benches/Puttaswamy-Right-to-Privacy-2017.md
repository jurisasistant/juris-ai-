# 🏛️ Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India (2017)
*Supreme Court of India • 9-Judge Bench • (2017) 10 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[DPDP-Act-2023-Digital-Personal-Data-Protection]] • [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] • [[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
A historic 9-Judge Constitution Bench ruling unanimously declaring that the **Right to Privacy** is a Fundamental Right guaranteed under **[[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]** and Part III of the Constitution of India.

---

## ⚖️ Key Facts & Constitutional Issues
* **Facts:** Challenge to the statutory constitutional validity of the Aadhaar biometric database and state surveillance of personal data.
* **Core Issue:** Whether the Indian Constitution protects a Fundamental Right to Privacy, overruling earlier 6- and 8-judge bench decisions in *M.P. Sharma (1954)* and *Kharak Singh (1962)*.

---

## 🏛️ Judgment & Ratio Decidendi
1. **Intrinsic Fundamental Right:** Privacy is an intrinsic element of the Right to Life and Personal Liberty under Article 21 and the freedoms guaranteed by Part III.
2. **Three-Prong Proportionality Test:** Any state restriction on personal privacy must satisfy:
   * **Legality:** Existence of a valid statutory law.
   * **Legitimate State Aim:** Need for state intervention.
   * **Proportionality:** Rational nexus between object and means, ensuring least intrusive measures.
3. **Statutory Shift:** Directly mandated the enactment of a comprehensive data protection regime, culminating in the **[[DPDP-Act-2023-Digital-Personal-Data-Protection]]**.
