# 🏛️ Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997)
*Supreme Court of India • 3-Judge Bench • (1997) 6 SCC 241*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[POSH-Act-2013]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
Supreme Court exercised its constitutional powers under Article 141 to lay down binding **Vishaka Guidelines** to protect women from sexual harassment at workplaces until statutory legislation was enacted.

---

## ⚖️ Key Facts & Constitutional Issues
* **Facts:** Public Interest Litigation filed after the brutal gang rape of social worker Bhanwari Devi in Rajasthan.
* **Core Issue:** Absence of statutory law in India prohibiting workplace sexual harassment, violating Fundamental Rights under Articles 14, 15, and 21.

---

## 🏛️ Judgment & Ratio Decidendi
1. Workplace sexual harassment is a direct violation of gender equality under [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] and the right to live and work with dignity under [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]].
2. Mandated every employer to establish an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
3. These guidelines formed the bedrock of the **[[POSH-Act-2013]]** (*Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013*).
