## 📌 Statutory Rules (Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2)
The civil court is empowered to grant a temporary injunction to restrain waste, alienation, or breach of contract pending the disposal of a suit.

---

## 🏛️ The Three-Prong Injunction Test
In **Dalpat Kumar v. Prahlad Singh (SC 1992)**, the Supreme Court laid down the authoritative three-prong test required to obtain a temporary injunction:
1. **Prima Facie Case:** There is a bona fide dispute and a substantial question to be investigated.
2. **Balance of Convenience:** Comparative mischief or hardship which will be caused to the applicant if the injunction is refused weighs heavier than that caused to the opposite party if granted.
3. **Irreparable Injury:** The applicant will suffer injury that cannot be compensated in money damages.
