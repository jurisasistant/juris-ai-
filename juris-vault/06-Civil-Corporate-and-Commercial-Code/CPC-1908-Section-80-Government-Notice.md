# ⚖️ CPC 1908 Section 80: Statutory Notice Before Suing Government
*Code of Civil Procedure 1908 • Mandatory Pre-Action Notice*
**Connected Nodes:** [[CPC-1908-Order-XXXIX-Temporary-Injunctions]] • [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandate (Section 80 CPC)
> *"No suit shall be instituted against the Government or against a public officer in respect of any act purporting to be done by such public officer in his official capacity, until the expiration of two months next after notice in writing has been delivered to, or left at the office of, the Secretary to that Government or the public officer."*

---

## ⚖️ Statutory Exception (Section 80(2))
A suit to obtain urgent or immediate relief against the Government or public officer may be instituted, with the leave of the Court, without serving any two-month notice. However, no interim injunction can be granted without giving the Government an opportunity of being heard.
