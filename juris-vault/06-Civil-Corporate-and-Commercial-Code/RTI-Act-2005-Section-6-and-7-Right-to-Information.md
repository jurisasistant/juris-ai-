# ⚖️ RTI Act 2005 Section 6 & 7: Right to Information & Mandatory Timelines
*Right to Information Act 2005 • Administrative Transparency*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]] • [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 6, 7 & 20)
* **Section 6(1):** A person who desires to obtain any information under this Act shall make a request in writing or through electronic means accompanied by the prescribed ₹10 fee.
* **Section 7(1) (30-Day Mandatory Timeline):** The Public Information Officer (PIO) must provide the requested information within **thirty (30) days** of receipt. If the information concerns the life or liberty of a person, it must be provided within **48 hours**.
* **Section 20 (Penalties):** The Information Commission can impose a penalty of **₹250 per day** up to a maximum of **₹25,000** on a PIO personally for refusing or delaying information without reasonable cause.

---

## 🏛️ Constitutional Foundation
* Operates directly under **[[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]]** (*Raj Narain v. State of UP, 1975*); the right to know is implicit in the freedom of speech and expression.
