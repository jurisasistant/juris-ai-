# ⚖️ Indian Contract Act Section 56: Doctrine of Frustration (Force Majeure)
*Indian Contract Act 1872 • Commercial Impossibility*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Indian-Contract-Act-Section-74-Liquidated-Damages]] • [[Specific-Relief-Act-Section-10-Specific-Performance]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (Section 56)
> *"An agreement to do an act impossible in itself is void. A contract to do an act which, after the contract is made, becomes impossible, or, by reason of some event which the promisor could not prevent, unlawful, becomes void when the act becomes impossible or unlawful."*

---

## 🏛️ Supreme Court Precedents
* **Satyabrata Ghose v. Mugneeram Bangur (SC 1954):** Indian law of frustration under Section 56 is statutory and positive; it applies wherever performance becomes physically or commercially impossible without fault of either party.
* **Energy Watchdog v. CERC (SC 2017):** Mere commercial hardship or unexpected rise in prices does not trigger Section 56 unless performance is totally frustrated.
