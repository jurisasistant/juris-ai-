# ⚖️ Companies Act 2013 Section 166: Statutory Fiduciary Duties of Directors

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 166)
1. **Duty to Act in Good Faith:** A director must act in good faith to promote the objects of the company for the benefit of its members as a whole, and in the best interests of the company, its employees, shareholders, community, and the environment.
2. **Duty of Care & Skill:** Must exercise independent judgment and reasonable care, skill, and diligence.
3. **No Secret Profit:** Prohibited from making direct or indirect secret profits or assigning office.
