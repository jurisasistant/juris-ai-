# ⚖️ Indian Stamp Act 1899 Section 35: Inadmissibility of Unstamped Instruments
*Indian Stamp Act 1899 • Evidentiary Admissibility & Impounding*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Registration-Act-1908-Section-17]] • [[Arbitration-Act-1996-Section-34-Award-Challenge]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 33 & 35)
* **Section 33:** Every person having by law or consent of parties authority to receive evidence shall impound any instrument produced before him if it appears not to be duly stamped.
* **Section 35:** No instrument chargeable with duty shall be admitted in evidence for any purpose by any person having authority to receive evidence, or shall be acted upon, registered, or authenticated, unless such instrument is duly stamped.

## 🏛️ Supreme Court 7-Judge Bench Precedent
* **NN Global Mercantile v. Indo Unique Flame (SC 7-Judge Bench 2023):** Clarified arbitration admissibility in unstamped contracts—while an arbitration agreement is an independent agreement, stamp duty defects on the principal contract must be cured by impounding and payment of duty/penalty before substantive enforcement.
