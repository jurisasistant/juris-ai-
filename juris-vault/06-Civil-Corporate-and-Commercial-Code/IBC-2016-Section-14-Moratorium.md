# ⚖️ IBC 2016 Section 14: Statutory CIRP Moratorium

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 14)
Upon admission of an application under Section 7, 9, or 10 of the IBC, the Adjudicating Authority (NCLT) shall by order declare a statutory moratorium prohibiting:
1. The institution of suits or continuation of pending suits or proceedings against the corporate debtor.
2. Transferring, encumbering, alienating, or disposing of by the corporate debtor any of its assets or legal rights.
3. Any action to foreclose, recover, or enforce any security interest created by the corporate debtor.

---

## 🏛️ Supreme Court Precedents
* **Swiss Ribbons Pvt. Ltd. v. Union of India (SC 2019):** Upheld the constitutional validity of IBC 2016; CIRP is designed to reorganize and save the corporate debtor.
* **P. Mohanraj v. Shah Brothers Ispat (SC 2021):** Moratorium under Section 14 applies to pending Section 138 Cheque Bounce criminal proceedings against the corporate debtor entity.
