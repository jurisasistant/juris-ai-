# ⚖️ Companies Act 2013 Section 188: Related Party Transactions (RPT)
*Companies Act 2013 • Board Accountability*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Companies-Act-2013-Section-166-Fiduciary-Duties]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 188)
* No company shall enter into any contract or arrangement with a related party (with respect to sale, purchase, leasing, or appointment) except with the consent of the Board of Directors given by a resolution at a meeting of the Board.
* **Arm's Length Exception:** Does not apply to transactions entered into in the ordinary course of business at arm's length price.
* **Audit Committee Oversight:** Governed by mandatory Audit Committee approval under Section 177.
