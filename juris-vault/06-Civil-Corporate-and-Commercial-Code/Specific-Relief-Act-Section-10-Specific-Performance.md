# ⚖️ Specific Relief Act 1963 Section 10: Mandatory Specific Performance
*Specific Relief Act 1963 (as amended in 2018) • Commercial Contracts*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Indian-Contract-Act-Section-74-Liquidated-Damages]] • [[CPC-1908-Order-XXXIX-Temporary-Injunctions]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandate (Section 10 — 2018 Amendment)
> *"The specific performance of a contract shall be enforced by the court subject to the provisions contained in sub-section (2) of section 11, section 14 and section 16."*

---

## ⚖️ Statutory Shift
* **From Discretionary to Mandatory:** Prior to 2018, specific performance was a discretionary equitable remedy. Under amended Section 10, Indian civil courts **must ("shall") enforce specific performance** as a general rule, unless the contract falls under limited statutory exceptions in Section 14 (such as contracts involving personal qualifications or continuous supervision).
