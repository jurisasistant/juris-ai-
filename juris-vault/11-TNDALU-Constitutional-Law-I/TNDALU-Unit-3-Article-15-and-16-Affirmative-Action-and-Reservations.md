## 📌 Affirmative Action & Equality of Opportunity
* **Article 15(4) & (5):** Special provisions for socially and educationally backward classes / SCs & STs (*State of Madras v. Champakam Dorairajan SC 1951*).
* **Article 16(4) & (4A):** Reservations in appointments and promotions in public employment.

---

## 🏛️ Supreme Court 9-Judge Benchmark
* **Indra Sawhney v. Union of India (SC 9-Judge Mandal Commission Bench 1992):**
  1. Enforced the **50% maximum reservation ceiling**.
  2. Mandated the exclusion of the **"Creamy Layer"** from OBC reservation.
  3. Prohibited reservation in promotions (subsequently modified by constitutional amendments).
