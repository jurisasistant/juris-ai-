## 📌 Preamble of the Constitution
> *"We, the people of India, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic and to secure to all its citizens: Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity..."*

---

## ⚖️ Judicial Status of the Preamble
* **In re Berubari Union (SC 8-Judge Bench 1960):** Initially held that the Preamble is not a substantive part of the Constitution and cannot confer powers.
* **[[Kesavananda-Bharati-v-State-of-Kerala-1973]]:** 13-Judge Bench overruled *Berubari*, establishing that the Preamble **is a part of the Constitution** and serves as an interpretive key to the Basic Structure.
* **S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (SC 9-Judge Bench 1994):** Secularism declared an inviolable basic feature under the **[[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]]**.
