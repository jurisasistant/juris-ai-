## 📌 Syllabus Focus: Rights of Minorities to Administer Educational Institutions
* **Article 29:** Protection of interests of minorities having a distinct language, script, or culture.
* **Article 30:** Right of all minorities (religious or linguistic) to establish and administer educational institutions of their choice.
* **T.M.A. Pai Foundation v. State of Karnataka (SC 11-Judge Bench 2002):** Landmark authority defining autonomy of unaided minority and non-minority educational institutions.
* **P.A. Inamdar v. State of Maharashtra (SC 2005):** State cannot impose reservation policy on unaided private minority educational institutions.
