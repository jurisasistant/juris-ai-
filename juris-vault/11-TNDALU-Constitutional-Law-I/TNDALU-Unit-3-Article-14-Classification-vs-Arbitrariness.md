## 📌 Syllabus Focus: Evolution of Article 14
* **Traditional Doctrine (Reasonable Classification):** Requires intelligible differentia and rational nexus to statutory object.
* **New Doctrine of Arbitrariness (*E.P. Royappa 1974*, *Maneka Gandhi 1978*):** Equality is dynamic; arbitrariness is the sworn enemy of equality. Wherever state action is arbitrary, Article 14 is violated immediately.
