## 📌 Three Foundational Constitutional Doctrines
1. **Doctrine of Severability (A.K. Gopalan SC 1950):** If an unconstitutional section can be severed from valid sections without destroying the statute's legislative intent, only the offending section is void.
2. **Doctrine of Eclipse (Bhikaji Narain SC 1955):** Pre-constitutional laws violating Part III are eclipsed; they revive automatically if a constitutional amendment removes the violation.
3. **Doctrine of Waiver (Basheshar Nath v. CIT SC 1959):** An Indian citizen **cannot waive** their Fundamental Rights conferred by Part III of the Constitution.
