## 📌 Syllabus Focus: Essential Religious Practices & Conscience
* **Essential Religious Practices Test:** *Shirur Mutt Case (SC 1954)* — Article 25 protects only core religious tenets.
* **National Anthem Case:** *Bijoe Emmanuel v. State of Kerala (SC 1986)* — Expulsion of Jehovah's Witness students for refusing to sing National Anthem (while standing respectfully) violated Article 19(1)(a) and Article 25 freedom of conscience.
* **Sabarimala Ruling:** *Indian Young Lawyers Association v. State of Kerala (SC 2018)* — Ban on entry of women aged 10–50 violated Article 14 and Article 25.
