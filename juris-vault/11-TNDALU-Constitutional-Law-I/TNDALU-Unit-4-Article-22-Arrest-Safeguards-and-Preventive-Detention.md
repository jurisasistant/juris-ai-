## 📌 Punitive vs. Preventive Detention
* **Article 22(1) & (2):** Right to be informed of grounds, right to counsel, and mandatory 24-hour presentation before Judicial Magistrate (*D.K. Basu Guidelines SC 1997*; *BNSS 2023 Section 58*).
* **Article 22(4) to (7):** Constitutional safeguards governing Preventive Detention laws (maximum 3 months without Advisory Board confirmation).
