## 📌 Detailed TNDALU Syllabus Doctrine
Expands the definition of "State" under Article 12 to include public corporations, societies, and autonomous bodies that are instrumentalities or agencies of government.

---

## 🏛️ Governing Precedents
* **Ajay Hasia v. Khalid Mujib (SC Constitution Bench 1981):** 6-factor instrumentality test.
* **Pradeep Kumar Biswas v. IICB (SC 7-Judge Bench 2002):** Reaffirmed financial, functional, and administrative state control test.
* **Zee Telefilms v. Union of India (SC 5-Judge Bench 2005):** BCCI is not "State" under Article 12, but performs public duties amenable to Article 226 writ jurisdiction.
