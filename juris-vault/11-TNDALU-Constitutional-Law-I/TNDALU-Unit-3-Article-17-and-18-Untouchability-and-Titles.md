## 📌 Abolition of Untouchability & Titles
* **Article 17:** Absolute prohibition of Untouchability in any form (enforced via Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955 and SC/ST Atrocities Act 1989).
* **Article 18:** Abolition of state-conferred aristocratic titles.
* **Balaji Raghavan v. Union of India (SC Constitution Bench 1996):** Ruled that National Awards (Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan/Bhushan/Shri) are not "titles" within the meaning of Article 18, but cannot be used as prefixes or suffixes.
