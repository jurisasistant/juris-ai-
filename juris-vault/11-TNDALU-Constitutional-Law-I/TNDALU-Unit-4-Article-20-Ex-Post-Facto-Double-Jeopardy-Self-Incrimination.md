## 📌 Three Inviolable Criminal Defenses
1. **Article 20(1) — Ex-Post Facto Law:** Applies strictly to substantive criminal punishment, not civil tax or procedural changes.
2. **Article 20(2) — Double Jeopardy:** Maqbool Hussain v. State of Bombay (SC 1953) — departmental confiscation is not a judicial prosecution; criminal trial can follow.
3. **Article 20(3) — Self-Incrimination:** Selvi v. State of Karnataka (SC 2010) — narco-analysis and lie detectors unconstitutional.
