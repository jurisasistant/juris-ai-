## 📌 Public Interest Litigation (PIL / SAL)
* **Relaxation of Locus Standi:** *S.P. Gupta v. Union of India (SC 1981)* and *Bandhua Mukti Morcha (SC 1984)* — any public-spirited citizen can invoke Article 32 or 226 on behalf of disadvantaged or illiterate groups.

---

## ⚖️ Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV — DPSP) & Fundamental Duties
* **Harmonious Construction:** *Minerva Mills v. Union of India (SC 5-Judge Constitution Bench 1980)* — The Indian Constitution is founded on the bedrock of the balance between Part III Fundamental Rights and Part IV DPSP. Neither can destroy the other under the **[[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]]**.
* **Fundamental Duties (Article 51A):** Added by 42nd Amendment Act 1976; 11 citizen civic duties.
