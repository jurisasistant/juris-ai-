## 📌 Syllabus Focus: Freedom of Press & Digital Speech
* **Freedom of Press:** Though not separately mentioned, press freedom is an integral part of Article 19(1)(a) (*Romesh Thappar SC 1950*, *Sakal Papers SC 1962*, *Bennett Coleman SC 1973*).
* **Right to Information:** Implicit in Article 19(1)(a) (*Raj Narain SC 1975*).
* **Digital Speech & Section 66A:** Struck down in **[[Shreya-Singhal-v-Union-of-India-2015]]**.
