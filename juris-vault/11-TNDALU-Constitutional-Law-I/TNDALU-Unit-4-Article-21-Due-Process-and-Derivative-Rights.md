## 📌 Syllabus Focus: Derivative Rights under Article 21
Following the transition to due process in **[[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]]**, TNDALU Constitutional Law I examines derivative rights:
1. **Right to Livelihood:** *Olga Tellis v. Bombay Municipal Corporation (SC Constitution Bench 1985 — Pavement Dwellers Case)*.
2. **Right to Live with Human Dignity:** *Francis Coralie SC 1981*.
3. **Right to Clean Environment:** *M.C. Mehta* series.
4. **Right to Privacy:** *[[Puttaswamy-Right-to-Privacy-2017]]* (9-Judge Bench).
5. **Right to Education:** *Article 21A* (86th Amendment Act 2002; *Unni Krishnan SC 1993*).
