## 📌 Union and its Territory (Articles 1–4)
* **Article 1:** India, that is Bharat, shall be a **Union of States**.
* **Article 3:** Empowers Parliament to form new States, alter areas, boundaries, or names of existing States by law (*State Reorganization*).
* **In re Berubari Union (SC 1960):** Cession of Indian territory to a foreign state requires a Constitutional Amendment under Article 368.

---

## ⚖️ Citizenship (Articles 5–11 & Citizenship Act 1955)
* Governs citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution (Article 5) and empowers Parliament to regulate citizenship by law (Article 11).
* **Citizenship Act 1955:** 5 modes of acquisition (Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalization, Incorporation of Territory) and Overseas Citizenship of India (OCI).
