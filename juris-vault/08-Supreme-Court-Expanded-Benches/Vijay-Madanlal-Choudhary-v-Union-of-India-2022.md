# 🏛️ Vijay Madanlal Choudhary v. Union of India (2022)
*Supreme Court of India • 3-Judge Bench • 2022 SCC OnLine SC 929*
**Connected Nodes:** [[PMLA-2002-Section-3-and-45-Money-Laundering-and-Bail]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[BNSS-2023-Section-480-Regular-Bail]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & PMLA Benchmark
Authoritative 3-Judge Bench decision upholding the constitutional validity of the **Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA)**, including ED arrest powers, search and seizure, and the stringent **Section 45 twin conditions for bail**.

---

## ⚖️ Core Ratio Decidendi
1. **Twin Conditions Upheld:** Section 45 bail rigor (court must be satisfied there are reasonable grounds believing accused is not guilty) does not violate Article 14 or Article 21.
2. **ECIR vs. FIR:** An Enforcement Case Information Report (ECIR) is an internal administrative document; ED is not required to supply a copy of the ECIR to the accused at the time of arrest, provided written grounds of arrest are supplied.
