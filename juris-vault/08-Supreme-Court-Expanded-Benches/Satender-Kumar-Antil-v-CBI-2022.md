## 📌 Case Summary & Bail Benchmark
Authoritative Supreme Court ruling reinforcing that **"Bail is the rule, jail is the exception"** (*Gurbaksh Singh Sibbia, 1980*), establishing structured categories for bail processing to combat undertrial overcrowding.

---

## ⚖️ Four Bail Categories (Category A to D)
* **Category A:** Offenses punishable up to 7 years without arrest during investigation—bail applications must be decided expeditiously without remanding the accused.
* **Category B & C:** Offenses punishable with death/life imprisonment or special economic offenses—courts must evaluate flight risk, evidence tampering, and severity of punishment without mechanical refusal.
