# 🏛️ Lalita Kumari v. Govt. of U.P. (2014)
*Supreme Court of India • 5-Judge Constitution Bench • (2014) 2 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNSS-2023-Section-173-FIR-and-e-FIR]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
A 5-Judge Constitution Bench ruled unanimously that the registration of a First Information Report (FIR) is **mandatory** under old CrPC Section 154 (now **[[BNSS-2023-Section-173-FIR-and-e-FIR]]**) if the information discloses the commission of a cognizable offense.

---

## ⚖️ Core Rules & Zero FIR
1. **No Police Discretion:** Police cannot refuse FIR registration or conduct a preliminary inquiry to test the veracity of the complaint if a cognizable offense is disclosed on face value.
2. **Preliminary Inquiry Carve-Out:** Preliminary inquiry is permitted only in matrimonial disputes, commercial offenses, medical negligence, or corruption cases, strictly completed within **7 to 14 days**.
3. **Zero FIR:** Police must register an FIR irrespective of territorial jurisdiction.
