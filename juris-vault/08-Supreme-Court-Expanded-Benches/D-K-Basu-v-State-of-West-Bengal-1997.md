# 🏛️ D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal (1997)
*Supreme Court of India • 2-Judge Bench • (1997) 1 SCC 416*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[Article-22-Arrest-and-Preventive-Detention]] • [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
Laid down **11 mandatory guidelines** for police arrest and detention to prevent custodial torture and deaths, enforcing Fundamental Rights under Articles 21 and 22.

---

## ⚖️ Key Mandatory Guidelines
1. Police personnel must wear accurate, visible, and clear name tags.
2. Arresting officer must prepare a signed **Memo of Arrest** attested by at least one witness.
3. Arrested person has the right to notify a relative or friend within 8–12 hours.
4. Entry must be made in the station diary regarding the arrest.
5. Arrested person has the right to consult a lawyer during interrogation.
