# 🏛️ Sushila Aggarwal v. State (NCT of Delhi) (2020)
*Supreme Court of India • 5-Judge Constitution Bench • (2020) 5 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNSS-2023-Section-482-Anticipatory-Bail]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Constitutional Benchmark
A 5-Judge Constitution Bench ruled unanimously that the protection granted under **Anticipatory Bail** (old CrPC Section 438, now **[[BNSS-2023-Section-482-Anticipatory-Bail]]**) should not be subjected to any fixed time limit; it generally continues until the conclusion of the trial.

---

## ⚖️ Core Ratio Decidendi
1. No statutory time frame limits anticipatory bail under Section 438 CrPC / BNSS Section 482.
2. Life of an anticipatory bail order does not end when the accused is summoned by the court or when charges are framed, unless specific judicial reasons warrant duration restrictions.
