# 🏛️ N.N. Global Mercantile Pvt. Ltd. v. Indo Unique Flame Ltd. (2023)
*Supreme Court of India • 7-Judge Constitution Bench • (2023) 7 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Indian-Stamp-Act-Section-35-Inadmissibility]] • [[Arbitration-Act-1996-Section-34-Award-Challenge]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Stamp Duty Benchmark
A 7-Judge Constitution Bench settled a long-standing controversy over the enforceability of arbitration agreements contained in **unstamped or insufficiently stamped commercial contracts** under the Indian Stamp Act 1899.

---

## ⚖️ Core Ratio Decidendi
1. While an arbitration agreement is separable from the substantive contract, an unstamped agreement is inadmissible in evidence under **Section 35 of the Indian Stamp Act**.
2. However, the defect is **curable**: courts or arbitral tribunals must impound the instrument, direct payment of statutory stamp duty and penalty, and thereafter proceed with arbitration.
