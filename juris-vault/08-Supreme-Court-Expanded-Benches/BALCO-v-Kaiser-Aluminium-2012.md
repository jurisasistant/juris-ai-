# 🏛️ Bharat Aluminium Co. (BALCO) v. Kaiser Aluminium Technical Services (2012)
*Supreme Court of India • 5-Judge Constitution Bench • (2012) 9 SCC 552*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Arbitration-Act-1996-Section-34-Award-Challenge]] • [[Indian-Contract-Act-Section-27-Void-Non-Competes]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Arbitration Benchmark
A 5-Judge Constitution Bench overruled *Bhatia International (2002)* and established that **Part I of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 has no application to international commercial arbitrations seated outside India**.

---

## ⚖️ Core Ratio Decidendi
1. **Seat-Centric Principle:** The legal "seat" of arbitration determines the exclusive supervisory jurisdiction of courts.
2. **No Indian Court Intervention:** Where parties choose a foreign seat (e.g. London, Singapore), Indian civil courts cannot entertain Section 9 interim injunction applications or Section 34 award challenges under Part I.
