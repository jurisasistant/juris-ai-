# 🏛️ Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v. Kailash Kushanrao Gorantyal (2020)
*Supreme Court of India • 3-Judge Bench • (2020) 7 SCC 1*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BSA-2023-Section-63-Electronic-Evidence]] • [[BSA-2023-Section-61-Electronic-Records-Primary-Evidence]]

---

## 📌 Case Summary & Electronic Evidence Benchmark
Settled conflicting jurisprudence by holding that the production of an electronic certificate under Section 65B(4) of the Indian Evidence Act (now **[[BSA-2023-Section-63-Electronic-Evidence]]**) is **mandatory** for admitting secondary electronic records.

---

## ⚖️ Judgment Ratio
1. Where the original computer or device is produced in court as primary evidence, no Section 65B certificate is required.
2. Where secondary electronic records (CDs, prints, server logs) are tendered, a signed certificate by the person in lawful control of the device is a mandatory condition precedent.
