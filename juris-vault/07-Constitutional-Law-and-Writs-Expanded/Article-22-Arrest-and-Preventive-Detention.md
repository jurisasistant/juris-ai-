## 📌 Fundamental Rights on Arrest (Article 22(1) & (2))
1. **Right to be Informed of Grounds:** No person who is arrested shall be detained in custody without being informed, as soon as may be, of the grounds for such arrest.
2. **Right to Legal Counsel:** Right to consult, and to be defended by, a legal practitioner of one's choice.
3. **24-Hour Magistrate Presentation:** Every person who is arrested and detained in custody shall be produced before the nearest Judicial Magistrate within a period of **twenty-four (24) hours** of such arrest, excluding the time necessary for journey.
