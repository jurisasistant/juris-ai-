# 📜 Article 300A: Constitutional Right to Property & Eminent Domain
*Part XII • Constitutional Rights • Former Fundamental Right*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] • [[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]]

---

## 📌 Text of Article 300A
> *"No person shall be deprived of his property save by authority of law."*

---

## ⚖️ Constitutional History & Due Process
* **44th Constitutional Amendment (1978):** Removed Right to Property from Part III Fundamental Rights (Article 19(1)(f) and Article 31) and re-inserted it as a Constitutional Right under **Article 300A**.
* **K.T. Plantation v. State of Karnataka (SC Constitution Bench 2011):** Held that deprivation of property under Article 300A must satisfy **due process of law**—it must be for a valid public purpose and accompanied by reasonable compensation.
