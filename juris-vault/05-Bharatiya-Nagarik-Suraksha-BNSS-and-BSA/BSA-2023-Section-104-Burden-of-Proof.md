## 📌 Statutory Rules (BSA 2023)
* **General Burden (Section 104):** Whoever desires any Court to give judgment as to any legal right or liability dependent on the existence of facts which he asserts, must prove that those facts exist.
* **Special Knowledge (Section 109):** When any fact is especially within the knowledge of any person, the burden of proving that fact is upon him.
* **Presumption of Innocence:** Foundational constitutional principle under [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]—prosecution must prove guilt beyond reasonable doubt.
