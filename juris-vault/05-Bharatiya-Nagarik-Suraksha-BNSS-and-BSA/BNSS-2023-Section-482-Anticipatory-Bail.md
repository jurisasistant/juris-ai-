## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNSS 2023 Section 482)
> **Section 482:** When any person has reason to believe that he may be arrested on accusation of having committed a non-bailable offense, he may apply to the High Court or the Court of Session for a direction under this section that in the event of such arrest, he shall be released on bail.

---

## 🏛️ Landmark Supreme Court Constitution Benches
* **Sushila Aggarwal v. State (NCT of Delhi) (SC 5-Judge Constitution Bench 2020):** Ruled that the protection granted under Anticipatory Bail should not be limited to a fixed time frame; it generally continues until the conclusion of the trial unless specific facts justify duration restrictions.
