# ⚖️ BNSS 2023 Section 173: FIR Registration & e-FIR Procedure
*Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023 • Replaces CrPC Section 154*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] • [[Lalita-Kumari-v-Govt-of-UP-2014]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (BNSS 2023 Section 173)
* **Mandatory FIR Registration:** Every information relating to the commission of a cognizable offense must be recorded by the police officer in charge of a police station.
* **e-FIR (Electronic FIR):** Allows citizens to report cognizable offenses electronically via email or portal, provided the signature of the informant is taken within three (3) days.
* **14-Day Preliminary Inquiry:** For offenses punishable with 3 to 7 years imprisonment, police may conduct a preliminary inquiry within 14 days to ascertain whether a cognizable offense is disclosed.

---

## 🏛️ Supreme Court Constitution Bench Precedent
* **Lalita Kumari v. Govt of UP (SC Constitution Bench 2014):** Registration of an FIR is mandatory under old CrPC 154 (now BNSS Section 173) if the information discloses a cognizable offense; police cannot refuse registration on grounds of jurisdiction (**Zero FIR**).
