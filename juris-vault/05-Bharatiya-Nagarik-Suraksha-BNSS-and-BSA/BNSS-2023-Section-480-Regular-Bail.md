## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNSS 2023 Section 480 & 483)
> **Section 480:** Empowers High Courts and Sessions Courts to release an accused in custody on regular bail upon appropriate surety conditions, ensuring the accused does not tamper with evidence or threaten witnesses.
> **Section 479 (Maximum Undertrial Detention):** First-time offenders who have undergone detention up to one-third of the maximum period of imprisonment specified for the offense shall be released on bail by the court.

## 🏛️ Supreme Court Bail Precedents
* **Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI (SC 2022):** Laid down structured bail categories avoiding unnecessary undertrial detention; "Bail is the rule, jail is the exception."
* **Gurbaksh Singh Sibbia (SC 1980):** Fundamental liberty principles governing bail discretion.
