# ⚖️ BNS 2023 Section 303: Offense of Theft & Snatching
*Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 • Replaces IPC Section 378 & 379*
**Connected Nodes:** [[BNS-2023-Section-318-4-Cheating]] • [[BNS-2023-Section-308-Extortion]]

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 303 & 304)
> **Section 303(1):** Whoever, intending to take dishonestly any movable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent, moves that property in order to such taking, is said to commit theft. Punishable with up to 3 years imprisonment or fine or both.
> **Section 304 (Snatching):** Introduces a specific statutory offense of **snatching** (e.g. chain snatching, phone snatching on streets) punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years.
