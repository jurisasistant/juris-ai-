## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 308)
> **Section 308:** Whoever intentionally puts any person in fear of any injury to that person, or to any other, and thereby dishonestly induces the person so put in fear to deliver to any person any property or valuable security, commits extortion. Punishable with imprisonment up to 7 years and fine.
