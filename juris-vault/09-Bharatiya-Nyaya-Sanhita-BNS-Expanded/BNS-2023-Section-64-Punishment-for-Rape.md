## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 64 & 70)
> **Section 64:** Whoever commits rape shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not be less than ten years, but which may extend to imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.
> **Section 70 (Gang Rape):** Where a woman is raped by one or more persons constituting a group or acting in furtherance of a common intention, each of those persons shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than **twenty (20) years**, but which may extend to imprisonment for life, and fine.
