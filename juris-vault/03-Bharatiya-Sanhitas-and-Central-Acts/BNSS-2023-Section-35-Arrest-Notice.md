# ⚖️ BNSS 2023 Section 35: Notice of Appearance & Arrest Rigor
*Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023 • Replaces CrPC Section 41A*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]] • [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Provision (BNSS 2023)
> **Section 35(1):** The police officer shall, in all cases where the arrest of a person is not required under the provisions of sub-section (1) of section 35, issue a notice directing the person against whom a reasonable complaint has been made, or credible information has been received, or a reasonable suspicion exists that he has committed a cognizable offence, to appear before him or at such other place as may be specified in the notice.

---

## ⚖️ Old vs. New Law Comparison
* **Old Section:** CrPC Section 41A
* **Statutory Codification:** Codifies the mandatory Supreme Court guidelines in **[[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]]**—restricting mechanical police arrest for offenses punishable up to 7 years.
