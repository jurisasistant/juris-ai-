# ⚖️ Negotiable Instruments Act Section 138: Cheque Bounce Procedure
*NI Act 1881 • Commercial Debt Recovery & Summary Trial*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Indian-Contract-Act-Section-74-Liquidated-Damages]]

---

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 138)
* **Offense:** Dishonour of a cheque for insufficiency of funds is a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment up to 2 years or fine up to twice the cheque amount.
* **Mandatory 30-Day Notice Timeline:** A written legal demand notice must be sent via Registered Post within exactly **30 calendar days** of receiving the Bank Return Memo.
* **15-Day Payment Window:** The drawer has 15 clear days from receipt of notice to remit funds.
* **Interim Compensation (Section 143A):** Magistrate can order the drawer to pay up to **20%** interim compensation during trial.
