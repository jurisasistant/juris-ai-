## 📌 Statutory Mandates (DPDP Act 2023)
* **Affirmative Consent (Section 6):** Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and capable of withdrawal at any time. Must be offered in bilingual format (English + Eighth Schedule language).
* **Security Safeguards (Section 8):** Data Fiduciaries must implement reasonable technical safeguards to prevent personal data breaches.
* **Statutory Penalties (Schedule):** The Data Protection Board of India can impose penalties up to **₹250 crore** for failure to take reasonable security safeguards.
* **CERT-In Cyber Rules (2022):** Mandatory requirement to report cybersecurity incidents within **6 hours** of discovery.
