## 🧭 Master Navigation & Knowledge Nodes

### 📜 Part 1: Constitution of India (Bharatiya Samvidhan)
* [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] — *Doctrine of reasonable classification and non-arbitrariness.*
* [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]] — *Fundamental freedom of speech and expression & reasonable restrictions.*
* [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]] — *The central hub of Indian personal liberty, due process, and privacy.*
* [[Article-32-and-226-Constitutional-Writs]] — *The heart and soul of the Constitution; Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari.*
* [[Article-368-Amending-Power]] — *Parliament's amending power under Article 368.*
* [[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]] — *Inviolable basic structure of the Constitution under [[Article-368-Amending-Power]].*

### 🏛️ Part 2: Supreme Court Landmark Constitutional Benches
* [[Kesavananda-Bharati-v-State-of-Kerala-1973]] — *13-Judge Bench establishing the [[Basic-Structure-Doctrine]].*
* [[Maneka-Gandhi-v-Union-of-India-1978]] — *7-Judge Bench establishing just, fair, and reasonable due process under [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]].*
* [[Puttaswamy-Right-to-Privacy-2017]] — *9-Judge Bench declaring Right to Privacy an intrinsic Fundamental Right under [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]].*
* [[Shreya-Singhal-v-Union-of-India-2015]] — *Struck down Section 66A of IT Act for violating [[Article-19-1-a-Freedom-of-Speech]].*
* [[Vishaka-v-State-of-Rajasthan-1997]] — *Constitutional guidelines for gender equality in workplaces under [[Article-14-Equality-Before-Law]] & [[POSH-Act-2013]].*
* [[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]] — *Mandatory police notice of appearance before arrest under [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]].*

### ⚖️ Part 3: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023 Offenses)
* [[BNS-2023-Section-63-Rape-and-Sexual-Offenses]] — *Replaces IPC Section 376; gender justice and consent standards.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-69-Deceitful-Promise-to-Marry]] — *Separate statutory offense for intercourse under deceitful promise.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-85-and-86-Matrimonial-Cruelty]] — *Replaces IPC Section 498A; governed by [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]].*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-103-Murder]] — *Replaces IPC Section 302; statutory punishment for homicide & mob lynching.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-109-Attempt-to-Murder]] — *Replaces IPC Section 307; intention gathered from force and weapon.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-111-Organized-Crime]] — *First Central statutory codification of Organized Crime and syndicates.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-152-Sovereignty-Protection]] — *Replaces colonial IPC Section 124A (Sedition); targets secession and armed rebellion.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-318-4-Cheating]] — *Replaces IPC Section 420; fraudulent intention at inception.*
* [[BNS-2023-Section-356-Defamation]] — *Replaces IPC Section 499 & 500; introduces community service.*

* [[BNSS-2023-Section-35-Arrest-Notice]] — *Replaces CrPC Section 41A; codifies [[Arnesh-Kumar-v-State-of-Bihar-2014]].*
* [[BNSS-2023-Section-173-FIR-and-e-FIR]] — *Replaces CrPC Section 154; mandatory FIR and electronic e-FIR.*
* [[BNSS-2023-Section-480-Regular-Bail]] — *Replaces CrPC Section 439; Satender Kumar Antil bail rules.*
* [[BNSS-2023-Section-482-Anticipatory-Bail]] — *Replaces CrPC Section 438; Sushila Aggarwal 5-Judge Bench.*
* [[BSA-2023-Section-61-Electronic-Records-Primary-Evidence]] — *Elevates digital records to primary evidence.*
* [[BSA-2023-Section-63-Electronic-Evidence]] — *Replaces Evidence Act Section 65B; digital custody hash admissibility.*
* [[BSA-2023-Section-104-Burden-of-Proof]] — *Burden of proof and presumption of innocence.*
