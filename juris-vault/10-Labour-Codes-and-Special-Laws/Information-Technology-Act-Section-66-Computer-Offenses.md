## 📌 Statutory Penalties (Section 66 Series)
* **Section 66 (Computer Hacking):** Dishonestly or fraudulently modifying or damaging computer resources—punishable with up to 3 years imprisonment or fine up to ₹5 Lakhs.
* **Section 66C (Identity Theft):** Fraudulently using electronic signature or password of another person—up to 3 years imprisonment.
* **Section 66D (Cheating by Personation using Computer Resource):** Up to 3 years imprisonment and fine up to ₹1 Lakh.
