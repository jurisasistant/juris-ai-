# ⚖️ POSH Act 2013 Section 4, 9 & 13: Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) Inquiry
*POSH Act 2013 • Workplace Gender Equality & Redressal Procedure*

## 📌 Statutory Mandates & Timeline
* **Mandatory ICC (Section 4):** Every employer with 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Complaints Committee presided over by a senior woman employee, with 50% women members and an external legal/NGO expert.
* **Inquiry Timeline (Section 11 & 13):** Complaint must be filed within 3 months of incident. The ICC inquiry must be completed within **90 calendar days**, and the final inquiry report submitted to the employer within 10 days thereafter.
