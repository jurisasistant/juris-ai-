## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Code on Wages 2019)
* **National Floor Wage (Section 9):** Central Government fixes a national floor wage; no state government can fix a minimum wage below this statutory floor.
* **Prohibition of Gender Discrimination (Section 3):** Strictly prohibits discrimination among employees on the ground of gender in matters relating to wages for the same work or work of similar nature.
