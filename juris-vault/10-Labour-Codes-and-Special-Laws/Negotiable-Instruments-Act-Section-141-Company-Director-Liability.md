# ⚖️ NI Act Section 141: Joint Criminal Liability of Company Directors

## 📌 Statutory Mandates (Section 141)
> **Section 141(1):** If the person committing an offense under Section 138 is a company, every person who, at the time the offense was committed, was in charge of, and was responsible to the company for the conduct of the business of the company, as well as the company, shall be deemed to be guilty of the offense.
* **SMS Pharmaceuticals v. Neeta Bhalla (SC 3-Judge Bench 2005):** Simply being a director is not enough; the complaint must specifically aver how and in what manner the director was in charge of the company's business when the dishonoured cheque was issued.
