# ⚖️ Consumer Protection Act 2019 Section 34, 47 & 58: Three-Tier Pecuniary Jurisdiction
*Consumer Protection Act 2019 • Speedy Redressal & E-Commerce Liability*
**Connected Nodes:** [[Article-21-Right-to-Life-and-Privacy]]

---

## 📌 Three-Tier Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions
1. **District Commission (Section 34):** Pecuniary jurisdiction for claims where the value of goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed **₹50 Lakhs**.
2. **State Commission (Section 47):** Pecuniary jurisdiction for claims between **₹50 Lakhs and ₹2 Crore**.
3. **National Commission — NCDRC (Section 58):** Claims exceeding **₹2 Crore**.
* **E-Daakhil Portal:** Electronic filing of complaints from anywhere in India without mandatory personal appearance.
