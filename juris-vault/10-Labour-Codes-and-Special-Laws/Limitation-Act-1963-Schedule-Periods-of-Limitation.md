## 📌 Essential Limitation Periods in Indian Civil Litigation
* **Breach of Contract (Article 55):** Three (3) years from the date when the contract is broken.
* **Summary Suit on Bill of Exchange / Cheque (Article 35):** Three (3) years from the date when the bill/cheque falls due.
* **Suit for Possession of Immovable Property (Article 65):** Twelve (12) years when the possession of the defendant becomes adverse to the plaintiff.
* **Execution of Decree (Article 136):** Twelve (12) years from the date when the decree or order becomes enforceable.
